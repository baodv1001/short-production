# Genres and hooks

## Popular genres
- Revenge (báo thù): wronged person returns with power.
- Hidden identity (thân phận ẩn): poor person is secretly rich or powerful.
- Family drama (gia đình): mother-in-law, inheritance, secret child.
- Romance (ngôn tình): contract marriage, ex-lover returns, CEO.
- Time travel (xuyên không / trọng sinh): second chance, fix the past.

## Hook formulas (first 3-5 s)
- Start in the middle of conflict: a slap, a shout, a divorce paper.
- Show the shock image first, explain later.
- First line is a threat or a question: "Anh còn dám về đây sao?"
- Do not start with a long setup, a title card, or a quiet landscape.

## Twist rhythm
- Small twist every 3-5 shots.
- 2-3 plot twists for each episode.
- Last shot is a cliffhanger: new person, new fact, or new danger. Cut at the peak.
- The next episode must start by using the cliffhanger.

## Series arc (10-30 episodes)
- Ep 1-3: hook, injustice, first reveal.
- Middle: power shifts back and forth. One big reveal every 5 episodes.
- Last 3 episodes: main secret, confrontation, payoff.

## Dialogue rules (Vietnamese)
- Short lines. 5-12 words. One idea for each line.
- Use kinship terms in a correct way: anh/em, chị/em, ba/mẹ, ông/bà, con.
- Pronouns show relationship and power. A change from "em" to "cô" shows distance.
- One speaker for each shot. Split long exchanges into more shots.
