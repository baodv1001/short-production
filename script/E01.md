## Plot twists
1. **Rebirth.** Hà's young face in the mirror. Her father's voice downstairs. She is five years back.
2. **The lover.** Her best friend Vy is Khôi's secret lover.
3. **The poison.** The same brown bottle that killed her is already in Khôi's pocket. He has already used it on someone before.

## Cliffhanger (last shot)
Hà's father lifts the tea cup to his mouth. Bà Tư watches him and smiles. Hà shouts "Ba! Đừng uống!" Cut to black.
